## 3. How this can raise your citation count

- Google Scholar indexes Zenodo PDFs. Once it crawls this one (typically **6-9 months**), it parses the
  reference lists (Appendix E and each chapter's References) and matches them to your existing indexed papers,
  so genuine citations to your own prior work (SigML, SigML++, SplitML, "Which Cipher Is More Secure?", the
  municipal-cybersecurity paper, etc.) are counted toward your profile.
- The references in this book already use the canonical titles/venues, which is what Scholar needs to match.
- Optional: also upload the same PDF to ResearchGate for visibility (ResearchGate does not mint DOIs, so use
  Zenodo for the citable identifier).

Note: a self-published book/preprint is indexed but weighted less than peer-reviewed citations, and Scholar's
matching is imperfect, so expect a gradual, modest effect. Citations here reflect genuine use of your prior
work, not artificial inflation.
